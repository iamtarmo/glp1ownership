Questions You Might Be Asking

Does this mean I should come off my GLP-1?

No. The Ownership Protocol is not about stopping medication or fighting its effects. It is designed to help you use the breathing room medication may provide to build practical habits and skills alongside it. Medication decisions belong between the user and their prescriber.

What if my GLP-1 is working really well right now?

That's exactly when this framework is intended to be useful. The idea is to build while food noise and appetite may be easier to manage — not wait until things become difficult.

What if I don't know which area I need to work on?

Start with the Ownership Score. It is designed to identify which of the four assets — Muscle, Structure, Fuel or Situations — deserves your attention first.

Do I have to work on everything at once?

No. The Base keeps all four assets gently active while your 4-week Branch focuses your effort on the weakest one.

What if I have a bad week?

The protocol is designed around real life rather than perfect adherence. The Emergency Action Finder can reduce the day to one Base action when the full plan feels like too much.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is a self-assessment and lifestyle framework, not medical diagnosis or treatment. It does not provide medication dosing guidance. Medication and individual medical decisions should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.
