The Craving Response Card

Step 1 · Map your triggers

What reliably sends things sideways?

Think back to before the medication. Stress? Late nights? A particular kind of social event? Travel? “Stress eating” is usually too vague to be useful — look for the actual scene. Add up to three.

Step 2 · Build a response

Decide it now, not in the moment

For each trigger above, fill in exactly what you'll do next time it appears. Not: “I'll be more mindful.” Instead: “I'll take a five-minute walk before deciding,” or “I'll text a friend first.” The more concrete, the easier to use when your brain is already busy with the trigger.

Your trigger may show up naturally — if it does, use the plan. If it doesn't, create a low-stakes version on purpose and test it while the stakes are still low. Then come back and check “held under pressure” below.
