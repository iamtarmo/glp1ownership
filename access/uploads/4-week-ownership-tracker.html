After Week Four

Three things to keep indefinitely

  1. Protein at your first meal.
  2. Whatever your Branch built, at whatever frequency you landed on by week four — not the maximum, the sustainable one.

And If You Slip
The reset

Go back to the Base for one week. Exactly as written. No heroic comeback. No punishment. No “starting over Monday.” Just return to the four basics.

Most people find their way back within days — and the second time is usually easier, because you're not starting from zero. You're returning to something you've already built.
